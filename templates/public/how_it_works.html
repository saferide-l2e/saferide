How do I get ready to book?

Create a passenger account, sign in and complete your account profile. {% if automatic_approval %}Submit your profile to start automatic approval, which runs after five seconds. This is account access approval, not identity or safety screening.{% else %}Booking eligibility requires staff approval. Creating an account alone does not approve it.{% endif %}

What happens when I reserve?

Eligible published departures offer a 10-minute seat hold. Keep your private boarding code for the assigned driver, who checks it at boarding. If the hold expires before check-in, the seat can be released.

Where are my booking details?

Your boarding code, booking status and cancellation action are in My Trips. Never post your boarding code publicly.

Why are there no buses for my route?

Search only shows published departures. A route preview does not guarantee a bus or seat. Try another journey or return when an operator publishes a departure.
