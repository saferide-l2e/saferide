Know what SafeRide provides.

What does account review mean?

The current account profile process is a staff-gated eligibility review. It does not perform official identity, licence or criminal-background checks. Submit only the information requested by the form; do not upload identity documents.

View your account status →
How do I report an issue?

Sign in and submit a concern for staff review. Reports are private and are not shared publicly. This channel is not monitored as emergency dispatch.

What if I need urgent help?

Contact local emergency services or someone you trust directly. SafeRide does not provide emergency response, live GPS monitoring or insurance.
